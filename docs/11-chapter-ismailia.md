# 11 — Ismailia chapter

One governorate chapter of *Nile Grid*. It is the first map **being designed**, not a required first level: players may open any shipped chapter in any order ([10-storyline.md](10-storyline.md)). Map, scale, and real landmarks are defined in [09-ismailia-map.md](09-ismailia-map.md).

**Chapter hook (agreed):** Horus Corp controls the Suez Canal and has halted ship traffic. The chapter goal is to infiltrate the **Suez Canal Authority Ships’ Guidance (Irshad) building** on the north shore of **Lake Timsah**, restore canal traffic, and then open the canal mouth. The Corp’s Ismailia surveillance tower is mounted on that building in the fiction. This is not a statement about the real Suez Canal Authority.

**Inside this map, mission order is fixed: west → east** (objectives 1–6 below).

**First playable build (2026-11-27):** missions **1–3** only — the railway station / Orabi Square checkpoint, the Hay el-Afrang patrol schedule, and de Lesseps House / the canal museum routing key plus crossing the Sweet-Water Canal. Hazem will review that slice before missions 4–6 are built.

Crew names are **approved**: Nour Hassan (courier, male), Adel Mansour (mechanic, male), Laila Farouk (hacker, the only girl). Personas and the power matrix are in [12-characters.md](12-characters.md). Sample dialogue for missions 1–3, and the hybrid dialogue UI, are in [13-dialogue.md](13-dialogue.md). Gameplay is action combined with stealth and puzzle-solving.

## Chapter story

A freight town has been told to wait. Upstream and downstream, ships sit still. Horus Corp has taken the guidance system in the Irshad building, raised a fictional boom toward the canal mouth, and put the Eye on every quay. The old town south of the railway still has neighbours, tea, and tight streets. North of the railway, and along the water, patrols are thicker.

The crew arrives from the west, at **Ismailia Railway Station**, as if they were just another package. They will walk the real city from west to east: through **Hay el-Afrang**, down to the historic quay and **de Lesseps House**, across the **Sweet-Water Canal**, through **Mallaha Park**, into the **Irshad building**, and then out onto the lake and the canal itself — **El-Forsan**, the **Nemra 6 ferry**, and the **Battle of Ismailia Memorial** at the water’s edge. Taking the tower is the climax; opening the waterway is the ending. When the first ship moves again, Ismailia is free. The crew has weakened the Grid. Other governorate chapters remain open in any order; this map does not unlock or gate them.

## Geographic spine

Objectives follow real relative positions, west → east, south of the railway for most of the path, then along the lake to the canal. Distances are the real ones at the decided scale (~3.5 m/tile; the walk is long, and a minimap is required).

| Order | Area | Landmarks used (from [09-ismailia-map.md](09-ismailia-map.md)) |
|------|------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | West arrival | Ismailia Railway Station, Orabi Square |
| 2 | Old town / Hay el-Afrang | Gumhuriya Square, St. Mark Cathedral, Abu Bakr el-Siddiq Mosque; Mustafa Kamel Square on the eastern edge of the quarter |
| 3 | The quay | Ferdinand de Lesseps House, SCA 1920s administration building, Suez Canal Museum, Sweet-Water Canal at Bridge 3; the SCA Historical Exhibition and the Sailing Club sit just south, on the lake side of the canal |

Off-spine landmarks (Ismailia Stadium, Al-Ismaily Mosque north of the railway, the SCA Stadium east of Mallaha, the Sailing Club if not used in objective 3) are better as **side missions** or wayfinding, not as required main-path beats.

## Mission structure

Six main objectives. The first playable build ships **objectives 1–3**. In co-op, each objective splits into **deliver / hack / protect** tasks that can run in parallel, with stealth, action, and a puzzle beat. In single-player, see [Scaling](#scaling-one-player-now-multiplayer-later).

Combat and forced entry stay in streets, yards, service rooms, and corporate floors. **Mosques, churches, the museum, and the 1973 memorial are never raid targets** and must not be damaged.

### 1. The arrival — Railway Station and Orabi Square

The crew drops from a freight arrival into a watched station. The Eye sits on the Arab-revival façade and on the fountain in Orabi Square. A local contact in the Current — a station tea vendor — can point them south into the old town, but only if the checkpoint blinks.

- **Courier (deliver):** slip a pass, a package, or the team itself across the forecourt and into the first alley south of the square.
- **Hacker (hack):** blind or loop the station cameras for a short window.
- **Mechanic (protect):** hold the alley, jam a service gate, or keep a patrol from closing the square behind the runner.

**Win:** the crew is in the old town, south of the railway, without the quarter already being locked down.

### 2. Hay el-Afrang — Gumhuriya Square and the old quarter

Tight colonial streets, two faiths on neighbouring blocks, neighbours who still know the patrol times. **St. Mark Cathedral** and **Abu Bakr el-Siddiq Mosque** are wayfinding and civilian shelter only. The work happens in the squares and the streets: **Gumhuriya Square** (the heart of Hay el-Afrang) and, if needed, **Mustafa Kamel Square** toward the Greek quarter.

A fixer in the Current has a copy of the waterfront patrol mesh, or knows who does. The crew has to cross the quarter without turning it into a battleground.

- **Courier:** run a coded message or tag between the two squares, staying off holy grounds.
- **Hacker:** copy the patrol mesh from a street kiosk or a corporate junction box on Gumhuriya Square.
- **Mechanic:** cover the runner, and if a side street has been blacked out, restore a local circuit so civilians can move.

**Win:** the crew has a route and a timetable toward the quay, and the old town is still intact.

### 3. The quay — de Lesseps House, the old SCA buildings, Bridge 3

South of the old town, along Salah Salem Street, the historic quay still holds pre-corporate canal records and a **physical override token** — a fictional key that the Irshad guidance cab will later accept. It is hidden across **de Lesseps House**, the **1920s SCA administration building**, and the **Suez Canal Museum**. Treat the house and the museum as historic interiors to slip through, not to smash.

The **Sweet-Water (Ismailia) Canal** cuts this strip off from the lake. **Bridge 3** is the crossing the crew needs. The **SCA Historical Exhibition** and the **Sailing Club** on the south bank can support this objective or a side mission (a moored work boat, a second set of records).

- **Courier:** retrieve the override token from de Lesseps House (or the last pickup the hacker unlocks).
- **Hacker:** open and read the canal records in the museum or the 1920s admin building; decrypt the token’s handshake.
- **Mechanic:** repair or crank **Bridge 3** so the crew can cross the Sweet-Water Canal toward the park and the lake.

**Win:** the crew has the token, is on the lake side of the Sweet-Water Canal, and can approach Mallaha Park from the west.

### 4. The garden — Riad Square, Mallaha Park, Ismailia Museum

**Abdel Moneim Riad Square** sits between the old town and the waterfront. **Mallaha Park** is the last real cover before the Irshad building — trees, lawns, and corporate drones. The **Ismailia Museum** at the north edge of the park is a civilian garden pavilion: an optional help or a side mission (see below), not a required raid.

- **Courier:** scout and mark a quiet path through the park to the waterfront fence.
- **Hacker:** spoof or thin the drone net over Mallaha.
- **Mechanic:** hold Riad Square or the park gates so the team is not cut off from behind.

**Win:** a safe approach to the Irshad building is marked, and the park is still a park.

### 5. The Eye on the lake — Irshad building (climax)

The **Suez Canal Authority Ships’ Guidance (Irshad) building** — the long white modernist slab with the control cab at the west end — is the hero landmark of the level and the chapter boss site. In the fiction, Horus Corp has raised its Ismailia **surveillance tower** on this building and shut the guidance system. Infiltrate, defeat the fictional **tower warden** (an elite security unit plus the building’s defensive grid — not a real person), and restore guidance.

- **Courier:** reach the control cab and deliver the override token.
- **Hacker:** restore the guidance system and shut the local Eye so the tower stops calling the rest of the Grid.
- **Mechanic:** hold the lobby, stairs, or a service core so the other two are not boxed in.

**Win:** the local tower is dark, ship-guidance is back on, and the lake can talk to the canal again. This is the emotional climax. It is not yet the end of the chapter: a physical boom still blocks the mouth.

### 6. Open the waterway — El-Forsan, Nemra 6, the Battle of Ismailia Memorial

Guidance is not enough if a fictional boom and a jammer still close the eastern water. The last objective splits across the real eastern geography: the **El-Forsan** peninsula and beach in the lake, the **Nemra 6 ferry** on the Suez Canal (the eastern edge of the map), and the **Battle of Ismailia (1973) Memorial** on the bank where the lake meets the canal.

The memorial is a place of dignity. The crew does **not** fight on it, occupy it, or brand it. A civilian navigation beacon was silenced on nearby service ground; restoring that beacon is the respectful beat. The fight belongs at the ferry, the boom, and the jammer on Forsan.

- **Courier:** run the key or the charge to the boom / ferry lock.
- **Hacker:** kill the jammer on El-Forsan so the restored guidance can be heard at the mouth.
- **Mechanic:** clear or crank the physical boom, and cover the work on the beacon beside the memorial grounds.

**Win:** the first ship can move. Ismailia’s chapter ends. The Current can hold the quay. Other shipped chapters stay available in any order. If every shipped chapter is now complete, the pixel-art ending cutscene plays ([14-ending-cutscene.md](14-ending-cutscene.md)).

## Side missions (optional)

Small, local, and skippable. They should not be required to finish the chapter. Suggestions:

- **Sweet-Water Canal lift bridges** — restore a second green 19th-century lift bridge so neighbourhood boats can move, even if Bridge 3 is already open.
- **The Sailing Club** — recover a moored work boat for the Current; useful flavour if it was not used in objective 3.
- **Ismailia Museum** — help a fictional attendant keep a small collection out of a corporate “archive seizure.” No combat inside the pavilion.
- **North of the railway** — a neighbourhood blackout near **Al-Ismaily Mosque** (the mosque itself is not a stage). Restore a substation; do not stage a fight on prayer grounds.
- **The stadiums** — **Ismailia Stadium** or the **SCA Stadium** as an optional night gathering or a package drop, off the main west–east spine.
- **El-Forsan beach** — a stranded deckhand, or a civilian ferry, if the player reaches the lake early.

## NPCs

All fictional. No real officials, and no real historical figures as speaking characters (Ferdinand de Lesseps is present only as the historic house, not as a person in 2077).

Working examples, all changeable:

- **Umm Sayed** — tea vendor at Orabi Square; first contact in the Current.
- **Karim** — a fixer in Hay el-Afrang who knows the patrol times.
- **Hajj Rashid** — a retired canal pilot at the quay, who remembers how guidance is supposed to work.
- **Mina** — a gardener in Mallaha Park, who hates the drones more than the dark.
- **A deckhand on El-Forsan** — wants the beach and the channel open, not a speech.

Civilians should remain in the old town, the park, and the beach once an area is calmed.

## Scaling: one player now, multiplayer later

**First playable build (single-player).** Missions **1–3** only. All three approved characters are available. The player starts as **Nour** and may **switch at safe points**; off-role actions remain as slower contextual assists so nothing soft-locks (see [12-characters.md](12-characters.md)). Every objective in the slice must be completable alone. After Hazem reviews this slice, missions 4–6 can be built.

**Later co-op.** Two or three players split **courier / hacker / mechanic**. Tasks in each objective can run in parallel on the same real geography (one player in the house, one on the bridge, one on the door). Each client camera follows only its own character, as already decided. Nearby teammates render; far ones are handled by area-of-interest filtering once Colyseus exists.

**Failure and disconnects.** In solo, failing an objective retries that objective. In co-op, a dropped player should be able to rejoin (see [05-ux-and-loading.md](05-ux-and-loading.md)); the other roles can be temporarily assisted so the room is not stuck.

## What this file does not decide

- Tile windows for missions 1–3 at the ~3.5 m/tile scale (must be computed before the Tiled map is built).
- Enemy counts, timers, puzzle difficulty, and ability cooldown tuning.
- Final NPC names (the examples above are still working names).
- How the chapter select is presented (a map of Egypt, a board, or travel flavour). Chapters are non-linear; Ismailia does not have to be opened first.
