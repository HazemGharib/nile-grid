# Ismailia — visual reference notes

Photos are downscaled copies (≤1024 px) from Wikimedia Commons. Licence, author and page per file are in [`SOURCES.json`](SOURCES.json). CC BY / CC BY-SA require attribution; use them as **drawing references only**, never paste them into game art.

Removed: one Commons file titled "Suez Canal Authority Building" that actually shows the domed SCA building in **Port Said**, not Ismailia. Do not mix the two.

| Landmark | Files | Visual notes for the pixel artist |
|---|---|---|
| SCA Irshad (Guidance) building | `sca_guidance_1-3.jpg` | Long white modernist slab, ~9 floors, very dense grid of horizontal concrete louvres. Taller narrow tower block at the **west** end with plain white wall panel; on top a round glass control cab (like an airport tower) with dishes, antennas and a tall floodlight mast. A row of small vaults/arches along the roof edge of the main slab. At night lit white/blue. Surrounded by trees; red-roofed villas in front. |
| Ferdinand de Lesseps House | `lesseps_house_1.jpg` | Two-storey cream chalet, dark-green painted timber framing in the gables, steep pitched roof, wooden balcony on the upper floor. Tall palms around it; low white wall with white posts and green railing. |
| SCA 1920s admin building | `sca_admin_1920s_1.jpg` | Cubic three-storey block; olive/brown patterned walls with white frames, green shutters, white cornice and rooftop balustrade, small balconies. |
| Suez Canal Museum | `suez_canal_museum_1-2.jpg` | Long two-storey mustard-yellow building, white round-arched arcade on both floors, red-tile roof edge, large paved courtyard in front. Statue of de Lesseps on site. |
| SCA Historical Exhibition | `sca_exhibition_1.png` | Small cream villa with red-tile roof behind a stone base wall and black railing; blue fabric canopy at the entrance; big trees. |
| Quay villas (Salah Salem St.) | `quay_villas_1.jpg` | Two-storey villas, terracotta hipped roofs, cream walls, green wooden wrap-around verandas on both floors. Repeat as the "old Ismailia" house tile set. |
| Mallaha Park | `mallaha_park_1.jpg` | Dense tall casuarina and eucalyptus trees, open lawns, low fence along the road. |
| Ismailia Museum | `ismailia_museum_1.jpg` | Small cream/yellow neoclassical pavilion, columned porch, flagpoles, clipped hedges and big trees. |
| Railway station | `railway_station_1-2.jpg` | Wide low beige building, Arab-revival parapets, tall arched central entrance with clock; fountain on Orabi Square in front. |
| Abu Bakr el-Siddiq Mosque | `abu_bakr_mosque_1.jpg` | White mosque, twin tall slender minarets, central white dome with small side domes. |
| St. Mark church (Coptic Catholic, ex St. Francis de Sales) | `st_mark_cathedral_1.jpg` | Ochre/yellow brick Romanesque church, square bell tower with arched openings, round arched portal. |
| Sweet-Water canal | `sweet_water_canal_1.jpg` | Narrow canal with grassy banks, trees, green iron pergola/bridge structures. |
| Suez Canal | `suez_canal_1.jpg`, `suez_canal_2.jpg` | Wide blue-green channel, huge tankers/container ships, small SCA ferries; Nemra 6 corniche at night with lit apartment blocks. |
| Lake Timsah | `lake_timsah_1-2.jpg` | Calm light-blue water, sandy shore with palms, low resort buildings and clubs on the far bank. |
| Ismailia Stadium | `ismailia_stadium_1.jpg` | Yellow and blue stands (Ismaily colours), floodlight masts. |
| SCA Stadium | `sca_stadium_1.png` | Bright blue seats in a bowl, running track. |
| Battle of Ismailia memorial | `battle_memorial_1.jpg` | Giant concrete AK-47 rifle standing upright with its bayonet pointing to the sky (gift from North Korea), on a stepped base in a plaza with lawns, by the canal. |
